❓ Chapter 7: Beginner FAQ

▼

Q: How do students pay me?
Cash on Delivery when you hand them their bag at their hostel, or EcoCash/M-Pesa to your phone number.


Q: Can students see my profits or change prices?
Never. Student accounts have customer-only access. Only you have the secret passcode.


Q: What if I make a typo in a product name?
Go to Product Catalog, tap yellow Edit button, fix it, and tap Update.


Q: What if I mess up my catalog?
Tap the red button in Product Catalog: "Reset to Factory Catalog" to safely restore all 52 original items.


Q: Can I change product photos with my phone camera?
Yes! Tap yellow Edit on any product, tap Upload File, and take a photo with your camera. Photos are auto-compressed to ~70KB so they load fast and never run out of space.


Q: I see an order on the site, but no WhatsApp message arrived?
The student might have closed WhatsApp before tapping the green send button. Always confirm the actual WhatsApp chat message before walking to the hostel.


Q: What if a student isn't in their room during the delivery run?
Tap 📋 WhatsApp Delivery Roster in your Admin to copy all student phone numbers. Send a quick WhatsApp 15 minutes before: "On my way to your hall with your order!"
