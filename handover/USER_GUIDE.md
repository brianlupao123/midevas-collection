## 🛠️ CHAPTER 5: Step-by-Step "How To" Guides

### Guide 1: How to Add a New Product with Your Phone Camera
Want to sell a new dress, shoes, or snack you just brought to campus?

1. Go to **🛍️ Product Catalog** tab.
2. Tap the blue button: **+ Add New Product**.
3. Fill in the simple form:
   - **Product Name:** e.g., *"Gentleman Ribbed Knit Polo Shirt"*
   - **Category:** Select from dropdown (*Men's Fashion, Ladies' Bags, Perfumes, etc.*)
   - **Regular Price ($):** e.g., `8.00`
   - **Comrade Price ($):** e.g., `7.00` *(Student discounted price)*
   - **Short Description:** e.g., *"Comfortable ribbed polo, great for classes and church."*
4. **Add the Photo:**
   - Tap **"Upload Photo"**.
   - Your phone will ask: *"Take Photo"* or *"Choose from Photo Library"*.
   - Snap a picture of the item on a clean surface or hanger!
5. Tap **"Save Product to Catalog"**.
6. That's it! The new item is instantly visible on your live website for students to buy!

---

### Guide 1B: How to Change an Existing Product's Photo (Replace Stock Photo with Real Photo)
Want to replace a stock picture with your real tuckshop grocery photo or new clothing shipment?

1. Tap the **🛍️ Product Catalog** tab in your Admin dashboard.
2. Find the item you want to update (e.g., *Mazoe Orange Crush*, *Colgate Triple Action*, *Lays Chips*, or *Sun Jam*).
3. Tap the yellow **✏️ Edit** button next to that product.
4. Under the **Product Image** section, choose either:
   - **Option A (Camera or Gallery Upload — Recommended):** Tap **"Upload File" / "Choose File"**. Your phone will open your camera to snap a live photo of the real packaging or let you choose a saved picture from your photo gallery.
   - **Option B (Web Image Link):** If your supplier gave you an image link, simply paste it into the **Image URL** box.
5. Notice the **Auto-Compression Indicator**: The system instantly resizes and optimizes your photo into a crisp ~70KB file so it never eats up phone storage or slows down students' phones on campus WiFi!
6. Tap the blue **"Save Changes"** button.
7. Done! The product immediately displays the real packaging photo across your entire live storefront.

## ❓ CHAPTER 7: "Don't Panic" Troubleshooting & FAQ

### Q1: "I made a typo in a product name or price. How do I fix it?"
> **Answer:** Go to **🛍️ Product Catalog**, find the product, and tap the yellow **"Edit"** button. Fix the spelling or price, and tap **"Update Product"**. It fixes immediately!

### Q2: "A student messaged me on WhatsApp but cancelled before delivery. What do I do?"
> **Answer:** Go to **📦 Orders & Quotes**, find that student's order, and change the status dropdown to **`Cancelled`**. If you want to delete test orders completely, click the red trash can icon next to the order.

### Q3: "I added an item on my laptop, but when I open the site on my phone, I don't see it. Why?"
> **Answer:** Your store currently runs on your device's private storage (which is why it has zero monthly server bills!). 
> When you want to manage products from both your phone and laptop seamlessly, tell your developer to turn on the free **Supabase Cloud Sync** (all code and database tables are already written in your `DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md`).

### Q4: "I accidentally clicked something wrong and messed up the catalog. Can I fix it?"
> **Answer:** Yes! In **🛍️ Product Catalog**, there is a red button at the top called **"Reset to Factory Catalog"**. Clicking it will safely restore all 52 original products with their clean photos and baseline prices.

### Q5: "I forgot my passcode. How do I get into Admin?"
> **Answer:** If you ever get locked out, clear your browser history/cache for `midevas-collection.vercel.app`, and the passcode will automatically reset to the factory default: `mideva2026`.

### Q6: "Can I change pictures of products from my phone without any code?"
> **Answer:** **Yes, 100%!** In your Admin, go to **Product Catalog**, tap the yellow **Edit** button on any product, and tap **Upload File**. Your phone will open your camera to snap a live photo or let you pick from your gallery. Tap **Save Changes** and the new picture is live immediately!

### Q7: "I see an order in 'Orders & Quotes', but I didn't get a WhatsApp buzz. What happened?"
> **Answer:** The website logs every quote when a student taps "Send Order via WhatsApp". However, if a student closes WhatsApp before tapping the green "Send" arrow, the message won't reach your phone. **Always verify the actual WhatsApp chat message from the student before packing and walking to the hostel.**

### Q8: "What if a student isn't in their hostel room when I arrive for drop-off?"
> **Answer:** Before you leave your room, tap the **📋 WhatsApp Delivery Roster** button at the top of your Admin screen. It copies all student phone numbers and room numbers to your clipboard. Send each student a quick message 15 minutes before: *"Hi! On my way to your hall with your order in 15 mins!"*

### Q9: "What if a student only has a big cash bill or wants to pay in mobile money?"
> **Answer:** Always keep a small coin/cash pouch in your delivery bag with small notes ($1, $2, KSh 50, KSh 100). If they don't have exact cash, they can send EcoCash to `0774809479` or M-Pesa to `+254 748 094795` right in front of you upon inspecting the items!

### Q10: "Will uploading photos from my high-resolution phone camera make the site slow?"
> **Answer:** **No.** The system has built-in automatic photo compression. When you take a 10MB photo with your phone camera, the store automatically compresses and resizes it to a crisp ~70KB before saving. It loads instantly for students on campus WiFi or mobile data!
