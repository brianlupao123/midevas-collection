❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Beginner FAQ)

Q: How do students pay me?
Students pay you via Cash on Hostel Delivery when you hand them their bag, or via EcoCash / M-Pesa to your phone number (+254 748 094 795 / 0774809479).
Q: What if I run out of a shirt or perfume?
Go to Live Stock & Pricing in your Admin and type 0 next to that item. The website will immediately put a red "Sold Out" badge on it and stop students from ordering it.
Q: How do I add new items I bought?
Go to Product Catalog -> tap + Add New Product. Snap a picture with your phone camera, type the price, and tap Save!
Q: Can a student see how much money I make or change my prices?
Never. Student accounts have zero admin access. Only you can enter the private dashboard using your secret passcode.
