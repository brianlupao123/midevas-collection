## ⚡ 2. The Fast Command Bar ("Do More in 1 Click")

Located right at the top of your admin modal when logged in:

### A. Store Status Switch
- **Store Open for Orders (Green):** Visitors see you are ready for orders.
- **Out on Delivery Run (Amber):** Notifies customers you are walking campus dropping orders.
- **Store Closed (Red):** Pauses immediate expectations (e.g. during exams or late night).
- *Tap the button once to cycle through modes!*

### B. Free Campus Delivery Toggle
- Switch Free Hostel Drop-Off **ON** or **OFF** in one click.

### C. 📋 WhatsApp Delivery Roster Generator
When you have orders ready for hostel delivery:
1. Click **📋 WhatsApp Delivery Roster**.
2. A neatly formatted campus drop-off list is copied to your clipboard automatically.
3. Open WhatsApp and paste it into your delivery chat or personal notes. It includes:
   - Customer names & hostel room numbers
   - Item lists and selected sizes/colors
   - Order total in USD and KSh/ZiG
   - Delivery instructions and customer notes

### D. ⚡ Confirm All Pending Orders
1-click button to mark all new pending quotes as **Confirmed** without clicking each order one by one!

## 📦 3. Managing Customer Orders & Quotes

Click on the **Orders & Quotes** tab:
1. Every time a student clicks *"Send Order via WhatsApp"* or completes an express checkout, the order is logged here.
2. You see:
   - **Order ID** (e.g. `ORD-9821`)
   - **Customer Name & Hostel Location**
   - **Items, Quantities & Variants**
   - **Total Quote Amount** (USD & KSh equivalent)
3. **Change Order Status:** Use the dropdown next to each order to move it from:
   - `Pending` ➔ `Confirmed` ➔ `Delivered` ➔ `Cancelled`
4. You can delete test orders using the red trash can icon.

## 👗 4. Managing Products (Add, Edit, Price Changes & Photos)

Click on the **Product Catalog** tab:

### Adding a New Item (takes 10 seconds):
1. Click **+ Add New Product**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Product Name** (e.g., *"Ribbed Class Polo"*)
   - **Category** (Men's Fashion, Bags, Perfumes, Snacks, Hygiene, Groceries, Stationery, Essentials)
   - **Standard Price ($ USD)** (e.g., `6.50`)
   - **Comrade Deal Price ($ USD)** (e.g., `6.00` — leave blank if no discount)
   - **Badge** (e.g., *"Comrade Steal"*, *"Hot Deal"*, *"Under $5"*)
   - **Photo Upload:** Click **Upload File** to choose a picture directly from your phone's camera or gallery!
3. Click **Add to Catalog**. The product is live immediately!

### Editing an Existing Item:
1. Click the **Pencil (Edit)** icon next to any product in your catalog table.
2. Change the price, badge, description, category, or photo.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

### Out of Stock Items:
- Click the **In Stock / Out of Stock** pill on any item. It instantly disables purchase on the public store so customers don't order items you don't currently have.
