## 📦 3. Live Inventory & Rapid Price Controls (NEW)

Click on the **📦 Live Stock & Pricing** tab:
This gives you real-time command over every single item's stock count and prices with zero code:

### A. Automatic Stock Decrement
- Every time a customer completes an order via WhatsApp, M-Pesa, EcoCash, or Cash on Delivery, the ordered quantity is **automatically subtracted** from that item's live inventory count!
- When an item hits `0 units`:
  - It automatically shows a bright red **Sold Out** badge on the store.
  - The "Add to Quote" button is safely disabled so students cannot order depleted stock.
  - Comrades see a prompt to message you on WhatsApp to pre-order for your next restock run!

### B. Quick Stock Steppers
Beside each product in the live inventory table:
- **`[-1]`**: Decrements stock by 1
- **Numeric Input Box**: Type the exact number of units you currently hold (e.g. `15`) and click away — it saves instantly!
- **`[+1]`**: Adds 1 unit
- **`[+5]`**: Adds 5 units
- **`[+10]`**: Adds 10 units

### C. 1-Click Batch Restock
If you have multiple items running low after a busy weekend:
- Click the top button: **Restock Low Stock (+10 all)**.
- Every item that had 5 units or less is instantly boosted by +10 units!

### D. Inline Rapid Price Editor
- Want to adjust the regular USD price? Simply type into the **Reg Price ($)** box.
- Want to set a comrade discount? Type into the **Comrade ($)** box.
- Changes save immediately with a confirmation toast!

## 📋 4. Managing Customer Orders & Quotes

Click on the **Orders & Quotes** tab:
1. Every time a student clicks *"Send Order via WhatsApp"* or completes an express checkout, the order is logged here.
2. You see:
   - **Order ID** (e.g. `ORD-9821`)
   - **Customer Name & Hostel Location**
   - **Items, Quantities & Variants**
   - **Total Quote Amount** (USD & KSh equivalent)
3. **Change Order Status:** Use the dropdown next to each order to move it from:
   - `Pending` ➔ `Confirmed` ➔ `Delivered` ➔ `Cancelled`
4. You can delete test orders using the red trash can icon.

## 👗 5. Managing Products (Add, Edit & Photos)

Click on the **Product Catalog** tab:

### Adding a New Item (takes 10 seconds):
1. Click **+ Add New Product**.
2. Type the item name (e.g., *"Gentleman Ribbed Knit Polo"*).
3. Pick a category (e.g., *Men's Fashion*).
4. Enter your regular price and comrade price.
5. Upload a photo from your phone/laptop or paste any photo URL.
6. Click **Save Product to Catalog**. It is immediately live on your store!

### Editing Existing Items:
- Click **Edit** beside any product to change its description, photo, or category.
- Click **Delete** to permanently remove an item.
